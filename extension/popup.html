How PaidAttention Works

When you open a page, a 15-second countdown runs before the meter starts. If you close clickbait within 15 seconds, you pay $0.00. Once the meter reaches the page's cap (e.g., $0.015), it stops completely so slow readers never pay extra.

Clicking 👍 never costs you extra money! It simply rebalances your daily spending so the creators you liked receive up to 2x more, while pages you didn't upvote receive slightly less.
